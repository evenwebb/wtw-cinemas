How Deep Is Your Love plus Director Q&A

1h 33minDrama, Romance

Chef Xia Mingjun of a fashionable restaurant and his wife Lin Mo have been married for seven years. The previous passion and romance has grown dull and dry. Lin Mo uses every trick she can think of to restore their marriage, but Xia Mingjun ends up leaving the city without a word. A year later, the reborn Xia Mingjun worked as a diving instructor on an unknown island. He meets a woman named Bai Jie, and falls in love immediately. Suddenly Mingjun realizes, all the methods Bai Jie used to approach him were the tricks he used to pursue Lin Mo; could this familiar yet strange woman Bai Jie be his wife?

Director: Guo Junli

Starring: Duan Yihong, Wan Qian, Qi Xi, Yuan Hong, Wu Yue, Dai Xu
