A swashbuckling musical reimagining of Robert Louis Stevenson's classic pirate adventure. The story follows a young boy named Jim and his dad, Bertie, as they set off on a treasure hunt across the high seas. Along the way, they encounter sneaky pirates, a chatty seagull, and a very festive holiday surprise! The lively retelling is packed with original songs and laughs, celebrating the importance of family and listening to the ones you love most.

Starring: Andy Day
