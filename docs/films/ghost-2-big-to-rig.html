GHOST: 2 Big To Rig

Captured live over the course of GHOST’s two sold-out shows in Mexico City last year, 2 BIG TO RIG is the definitive document of the band's unforgettable SKELETOUR WORLD TOUR, presented in IMAX® in select locations. Featuring the only pro-shot footage of Papa V Perpetua and his band of Nameless Ghouls from the phone-free SKELETOUR, 2 BIG TO RIG immerses the audience in the GHOST live ritual, including exclusive behind-the-scenes and backstage access.

Director: Amir Juan Chamdin, Jim Parsons

Starring: Tobias Forge, Per Eriksson, Cosmo Sniderman, Hayden C Scott, Laura Scarborough, Olivia Morreale
