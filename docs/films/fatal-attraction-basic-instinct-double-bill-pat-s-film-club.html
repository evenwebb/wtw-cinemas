For the next pat's Film Club desire becomes obsession in this electrifying double feature of 1980s and 1990s thrillers. In Fatal Attraction, a successful New York lawyer's brief affair spirals into a terrifying nightmare when his lover refuses to let him go, turning passion into psychological warfare. Then, in Basic Instinct, a detective investigating a brutal murder becomes entangled with a seductive crime novelist whose intelligence, sexuality, and secrets make her both irresistible and dangerously unpredictable. Together, these landmark films explore the dark side of attraction, where temptation blurs into manipulation, obsession, and deadly consequences. Featuring unforgettable performances, razor-sharp suspense, and some of the most talked-about moments in modern cinema, this double bill is a masterclass in tension, seduction, and the thrill of not knowing who to trust.
