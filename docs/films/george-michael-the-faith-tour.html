George Michael: The Faith Tour

1h 53minMusic

A stunning delve into George Michael's 1988 Faith Tour, as shot live at the famous Palais Omnisports de Paris-Bercy. This exclusive footage, assembled from a 14-camera shoot captured on 35mm over two nights (30–31 May 1988), features the 24-year-old pop star at the height of his powers.

Director: Andy Morahan, David Austin

Starring: George Michael, Herb Ritts
